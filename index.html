Main Parts of an F1 Car

What each part does
Part What it does Why it matters
Front WingDirects air around the car and pushes the front downHelps the car turn into corners
Rear WingCreates downforce at the back of the carGives grip and stability at high speed
TyresThe only part touching the roadGrip, speed and pit stop strategy
Engine (Power Unit)Produces the power to drive the carDecides top speed and acceleration
BrakesSlow the car down very quicklyLate braking helps overtaking
HaloStrong frame above the driver's headProtects the driver in a crash
Steering WheelUsed to steer and change many car settingsGives the driver control from the cockpit
